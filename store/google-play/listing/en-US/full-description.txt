Build habits at your own pace with Oisiu, a habit tracker that works offline without an account.

ORGANIZE YOUR HABITS
Create habits and arrange them into groups and subgroups. Track completion, numbers, or durations to suit your routine.

TRACK YOUR DAILY PROGRESS
Record daily values and review previous entries. Use the calendar to explore your history and update your records.

UNDERSTAND YOUR PATTERNS
Explore your progress, streaks, and frequency to see how your habits develop over time.

KEEP CONTROL OF YOUR DATA
Your habit records stay on your device. Export your data as JSON for backup or CSV for use in a spreadsheet. Import compatible JSON files without overwriting existing records.

CHOOSE YOUR APPEARANCE
Use light or dark mode to match your preference.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
Start tracking without signing up. Oisiu has no advertising, analytics, or developer-operated backend.

Habit tracking works without an internet connection. Data leaves the app only through actions you choose, such as exporting or sharing, or through your device’s backup settings.
