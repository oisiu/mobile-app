# Oisiu privacy policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Oisiu is a local-first habit tracker published by Oisiu. This policy explains how the Oisiu mobile application handles information.

## Information stored by the app

Oisiu stores the information you enter on your device, including habit names and emoji, hierarchy, daily values, local dates and timezones, and application preferences such as appearance. The app also accesses a JSON file only when you explicitly choose one for import.

Oisiu has no account system, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or developer-operated backend. The Oisiu developers do not collect, receive, sell, or share your habit information or other personal data through the app.

## Exports and external services

When you request an export, Oisiu creates a JSON or CSV copy in the app's temporary storage and opens the device's system share interface. You choose whether and where to send that file. Oisiu does not receive it.

If you choose to share an export with a storage provider, messaging application, AI service, or another third party, that service processes the information under its own terms and privacy policy. Review exported information before sharing it. Your operating system may also back up application data according to your device and account backup settings; Oisiu's developers cannot access or control those backups.

Google Play and your device platform may independently process store, download, diagnostic, or device information under their own privacy policies. That processing is not performed by Oisiu.

## Security

Oisiu relies on the mobile operating system's application sandbox and does not transmit habit information to an Oisiu server. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device and keep exports only in locations you trust.

## Retention and deletion

Habit information remains on your device until you delete records or habits in the app, clear Oisiu's application storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling normally removes the app's local database. Copies you exported, shared, or backed up must be deleted from their destination separately.

Because Oisiu does not operate accounts or collect your information, there is no server-side account or personal-data record for the developers to delete.

## Children's privacy

Oisiu is a general-purpose habit tracker and is not directed specifically to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published in this file with a revised effective date. The policy will be updated before a released version changes how the app handles information.

## Contact

For non-sensitive privacy questions, [open an issue in the Oisiu repository](https://github.com/oisiu/oisiu/issues/new) without including personal information. For a security or privacy vulnerability, follow the private process in the [Oisiu security policy](https://github.com/oisiu/oisiu/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
